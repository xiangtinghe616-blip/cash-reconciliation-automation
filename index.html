v2 upgrade

Design boundary

The engine decides. The LLM supports.

The important design question is not whether AI can help. It is where AI should stop. In this project, the control-sensitive reconciliation decision remains transparent and deterministic. AI is used downstream, where the work becomes interpretive and documentation-heavy.

Decision layer

Deterministic reconciliation core

Responsible for matching, break detection, exception classification, and possible-match routing.

  • Compares bank statement and internal ledger records
  • Detects exact matches and timing differences
  • Classifies missing items, amount mismatches, and duplicates
  • Preserves transparency for control-sensitive logic
Support layer

LLM-assisted analyst handling

Reads the already-classified exception queue and drafts support content for analyst review.

  • Explains the likely issue in business language
  • Suggests a practical next step
  • Drafts analyst notes for follow-up
  • Adds risk/control considerations without overriding classification
Workflow architecture

A reconciliation pipeline with review points.

v2 is organized as a staged workflow, not a single matching rule. Clear cases are handled early. uncertain cases are routed for analyst review, and unresolved breaks are classified into operationally meaningful exception states.

01

Synthetic Data Generation

Creates bank, ledger, and manifest files with known expected outcomes.

02

Data Quality Checks

Flags invalid dates, missing references, missing currency, and malformed amounts.

03

Staged Matching

Runs exact, timing, normalized-reference, split, and mismatch logic in sequence.

04

Possible Match Queue

Routes lower-confidence candidate pairs into review instead of auto-clearing them.

05

Exception Classification

Translates residual breaks into categories with priority and recommended action.

06

LLM / Template Support

Generates analyst-support text only after deterministic classification.

07

Review Outputs

Produces CSV artifacts for analyst review and management visibility.

Worked example

Not every near-match should be auto-cleared.

A mature reconciliation workflow needs a middle state. Some records are too similar to ignore, but not strong enough to clear automatically. v2 routes those cases into a possible match queue.

Candidate pair

Possible match requiring analyst review

The bank and ledger records share account, currency, direction, and a close date. The reference is similar but not identical, and the amount is close enough to investigate but not safe to reconcile automatically.

Field Bank Ledger
Account ACC1002 ACC1002
Reference WIRE-REF2048 REF2048-ALT
Date 2026-03-17 2026-03-19
Amount $10,000.00 $9,950.00
System response
Step 1

Do not auto-clear

The pair is not treated as a deterministic match because the amount and reference do not fully align.

Step 2

Route to possible match queue

The system records a candidate rationale based on shared account/currency/direction, close date, and reference similarity.

Step 3

Add analyst support

The support layer drafts an explanation and recommends reviewing fees, partial posting, adjustment history, or source booking details.

Control point

Human review remains required

The workflow accelerates investigation but does not replace validation, escalation, or final sign-off.

Operational value

The value is not just faster matching.

The bigger value is turning exception-heavy work into a more structured operating process: fewer raw breaks, clearer review paths, better documentation, and stronger visibility into unresolved items.

Reduce first-pass manual sorting

Routine matches and obvious timing items are separated earlier, allowing analysts to focus on unresolved or higher-risk exceptions.

Make exceptions easier to manage

Breaks are translated into categories, priorities, review actions, and outputs that can be investigated rather than manually interpreted from raw rows.

Use AI without weakening controls

AI supports explanation and documentation only after the deterministic engine has already made the classification decision.

Closing thought

Reconciliation automation is strongest when it respects the workflow.

This case treats reconciliation as a control-sensitive operations process, not a generic row-matching exercise. The project shows how disciplined automation can reduce manual friction, make exceptions easier to review, and introduce AI support without making the control logic opaque.
